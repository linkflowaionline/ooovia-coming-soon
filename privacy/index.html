Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Contact: hello@ooovia.com

Pre-launch notice: OOOVIA is currently a developing service and is not yet operated by a registered company. This policy describes intended service arrangements. OOOVIA is an AI-powered social media profile intelligence service. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you use our website and services.

1. Who operates OOOVIA

OOOVIA is currently a developing service operated under the OOOVIA brand and is not yet represented as a registered company. The individual or legal person responsible for operating the service must be identified before commercial launch. For privacy questions, contact hello@ooovia.com. We will update this policy with the operator's legal name and business address when established.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use OOOVIA, we may process account details such as your name, email address and authentication identifiers; public social profile URLs, biographies, headlines, posts, videos and visible engagement indicators; information returned through an authorised platform connection; information and analytics exports you choose to provide; your industry, geography, audience and objectives; and limited technical, security and diagnostic information needed to operate the service.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide profile audits, assess profile positioning and completeness, research publicly available competitor information, analyse visible content activity, prepare recommendations and free or premium reports, maintain account security, support customers and meet legal obligations. We distinguish observed facts from estimates, AI interpretations and unavailable metrics. We do not present estimated figures as verified platform analytics.

4. AI processing

OOOVIA uses AI services, including the OpenAI API, to organise and analyse information and generate recommendations. Submitted profile information, relevant public research and customer-provided material may be sent to these providers to perform the requested service. AI outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed. We do not claim that AI can access private platform data unless that data is supplied through an authorised integration or by the customer. Provider-specific retention and processing settings must be verified before launch.

5. Public research and social platforms

OOOVIA may use public information and permitted search services, including Brave Search, to research profiles. Access depends on platform visibility, technical restrictions, API permissions and applicable terms. OOOVIA is independent and is not owned, operated, endorsed or sponsored by LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube or their parent companies. We do not promise access to private analytics or information that a platform does not make available.

6. Optional LinkedIn connection

Connecting LinkedIn is optional and takes place through LinkedIn's authorisation process. The initial integration is designed to request OpenID Connect scopes openid, profile and email. The information received is limited to the permissions actually granted. Basic sign-in does not automatically provide posts, impressions, reach, follower demographics or all member content. Additional access will only be implemented when LinkedIn has approved the relevant product and permissions. OOOVIA does not ask for your LinkedIn password. You may disconnect the integration; deletion of previously stored reports or personal information may require a separate request.

7. Service providers and sharing

OOOVIA expects to use Supabase for authentication and database services, OpenAI for AI processing, Brave Search for public information discovery and Hetzner for planned application hosting. These arrangements and the relevant provider entities, contractual terms and processing locations must be confirmed before launch. Providers may process information on OOOVIA's behalf to deliver the service. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to address security, fraud or legal claims.

8. Storage and international transfers

OOOVIA plans to host application infrastructure in a Hetzner European data centre. The selected location has not yet been confirmed. Other providers may process or store information in different countries. European application hosting does not mean that all processing occurs in Europe. Before launch, OOOVIA will document provider locations and apply appropriate safeguards for international transfers as required by applicable law, including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law where applicable.

9. Security

OOOVIA intends to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, which may include encrypted communications, authentication, access restrictions, database row-level security, server-side secret management, restricted administrative access, security monitoring and backups. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Actual production controls must be verified before launch.

10. Retention

Information will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations and protect the platform. Account information, audit submissions, original customer-provided reports, LinkedIn connection information, analytics exports and security records may have different retention periods. Exact retention periods and automated deletion procedures are still to be configured and verified. Customers may contact hello@ooovia.com to request deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

11. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent, disconnect an authorised account, or ask questions about processing. Contact hello@ooovia.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and will respond in accordance with applicable law.

12. Cookies and technical data

OOOVIA may use essential cookies and similar technologies for authentication, sessions, security and website functionality. Optional analytics or non-essential tracking technologies, if introduced, will be used subject to applicable consent requirements.

13. Children's privacy

OOOVIA is designed for adult individuals, professionals, creators and businesses. We do not knowingly intend to collect children's personal information through the service.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as the service, providers or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through the website or other appropriate channels. The latest version will be published at https://ooovia.com/privacy.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@ooovia.com. The operator's legal name and business address will be added when the business is registered.
